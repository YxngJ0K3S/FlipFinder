LEARN THE BASICS

Reselling guides

1. Research sold prices

Look at completed or sold listings, not just asking prices. Compare the exact model, size, edition and condition.

2. Calculate every cost

Include purchase price, platform fees, payment fees, postage, packaging, returns allowance and any travel costs.

3. Check demand and competition

Compare how many similar items are listed with how many have recently sold. A high asking price does not prove an item will sell.

4. Start small and stay safe

Never spend money you cannot afford to lose. Meet buyers safely, use platform payments and keep proof of postage.

5. Keep accurate records

Record purchase dates, receipts, sale prices, fees and refunds. Check current HMRC guidance if you trade regularly.
